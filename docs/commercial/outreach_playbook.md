## Section 3: Top 10 Merchant Objections & Answers (Objection Bible)

### 1. "Will asking for a ₹100 deposit make my customers angry?"
* **Answer:** *"Only extreme-risk orders (~15-20%) are routed to deposits. Borderline orders receive a friendly WhatsApp address check (₹2). For high-risk orders, honest buyers gladly pay ₹100 to reserve their product, while chronic fraudsters abandon. You protect ₹150 shipping cost with zero brand damage."*

### 2. "We already use KLIP / Shiprocket's risk score."
* **Answer:** *"Those scores give you a red/yellow/green badge or a blanket pincode toggle. They don't price the order. FlipPrice calculates whether the cart margin outweighs the logistics risk and recommends the exact lowest-cost action to maximize net rupees."*

### 3. "Do I need to change my Shopify checkout flow?"
* **Answer:** *"No. FlipPrice operates post-checkout via Shopify webhooks and tags (`fp_risk:`, `fp_action:`). It works seamlessly with Shopify Basic, Shopify Advanced, and Shopify Plus."*

### 4. "Is customer data safe?"
* **Answer:** *"All phone numbers and emails are salted and hashed with SHA-256 upon ingestion under DPDP Act 2023 standards. We never store raw customer PII."*

### 5. "What if WhatsApp confirmation messages don't get read?"
* **Answer:** *"Indian WhatsApp Business templates have an 85–92% open rate within 15 minutes. If a customer doesn't respond within your set window (e.g. 6 hours), the order can either be auto-held or dispatched based on your policy config."*
